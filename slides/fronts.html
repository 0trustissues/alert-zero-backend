02 · The platform · Elastic Security's answer

Block the known. Hunt the unknown.

Two ways Elastic Security answers those bottlenecks. Today goes deep on the second one.

Front 1 · Prevention

Block the known

Malicious behaviour is denied before it runs: at machine speed, across every endpoint.

  • Behavioural protections stop decisive actions at execution
  • One prevention architecture for Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Containment before the attacker gets to act
Front 2 · Hunting · The focus today

Hunt the unknown

Continuous threat hunting, powered by AlertZero, turns raw research into validated detection.

  • Reads threat research and pulls out the tradecraft
  • Runs grounded, validated queries against your environment
  • Correlates evidence into campaigns, or gives an honest no
  • Proposes new detection rules for a human to review
