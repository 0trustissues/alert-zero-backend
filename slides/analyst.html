01 · Why now · The analyst's day

The SOC hits bottlenecks at every turn.

Four things analysts tell us, in their own words.

Alert fatigue
I can't trust my own queue.

Thousands of alerts, mostly noise. The real attack is buried, and there aren't enough hours to tell which is which.

Intel lag
The blog dropped Tuesday. We're still building coverage.

Someone reads the research, writes the detection, and proves it fires: all by hand, while the threat is live.

Investigation toil
Correlating this by hand takes all afternoon.

Pivoting across endpoint, cloud and identity to prove one campaign is slow, manual and easy to get wrong.

No trust in AI
Why should I believe what the model wrote?

A confident answer with no receipts is worse than none. Analysts won't act on a black box.
