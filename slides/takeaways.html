05 · Before you go

Key takeaways.

01

The SOC hits bottlenecks everywhere. We covered two answers: blocking known threats at the endpoint, and hunting the unknown that never trips an alert. Both run on one platform.

02

Prevention cuts the alert load at the source. Every threat stopped before it executes is one fewer alert to chase and one fewer investigation to open.

03

AlertZero works the rest, and you stay in command. Four Watches triage, hunt, tune and investigate around the clock. You see the query and the evidence, and you approve every consequential action.

This is AlertZero: agentic capabilities across Elastic Security that adopt at your pace, keep you in command, and run wherever your data lives.
