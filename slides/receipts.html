04 · Trust and control · Receipts

Every finding carries its receipts.

Trust isn't added at the end. It is how each Worker is built.

01 · Evidence first

The model only judges

The workflow gathers alerts, entities and raw events. For judgement calls the model has no tools at all.

02 · No invented IDs

Checked before you see it

Attack Discovery drops any alert ID the model made up.

03 · A human's name

Runs as the approver

An approved action runs under the identity of the person who clicked Approve.

04 · Nothing waits forever

72 hours, then expired

Undecided proposals are recorded as expired, never quietly dismissed.

05 · Guardrails

Budgets and back-pressure

One sweep at a time per space, hard caps, and a Task Manager headroom check.

06 · The honest no

Unknown stays unknown

Missing telemetry is inconclusive, never false positive. Zero matches are recorded as zero.
